# I³ League

I³ League is a year-round student innovation platform for middle and high schoolers. Rather than a one-off science-fair submission, students work through a structured innovation journey on a real problem they choose themselves, working alone or on a team, and can qualify along the way for a national competition.

This site is the public face of the program and the home for registered students: it explains how the program works and what it costs, and gives each student their own account to track progress, submit work, and build toward certification.
