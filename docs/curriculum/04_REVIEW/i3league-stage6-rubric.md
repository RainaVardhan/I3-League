# i3League Stage 6 — INFLUENCE
## Advancement Review Rubric

**Required Artifact:** Final Pitch + Innovation Portfolio

**Stage Gate Question:** Can the team make a credible, evidence-backed case for what should happen next?

**Mental Test:** Explain → Show → Prove → Defend → Ask

**What reviewers are NOT judging at this stage:** We are judging whether the full journey is credible and defensible, not merely presentation charisma.

---

## 1. Evidence Quality

**Reviewer checks:** Does every major claim in the pitch trace to evidence from the Portfolio? Is at least one strong evidence visual included?

| Level | Descriptor |
|---|---|
| Not Yet Evident | Major claims are unsupported, or the pitch relies on assertion rather than evidence from the Portfolio. |
| Developing | Some claims trace to evidence, but key claims are unsupported or the connection is unclear. |
| Meets Standard | Every major claim clearly traces to specific evidence in the Portfolio, including at least one clear evidence visual. |
| Strong Evidence | Claims and evidence are tightly and visibly linked throughout, and the visual evidence makes the strongest result immediately understandable. |

**Load-bearing requirement:** Every major claim must trace to evidence. A pitch cannot advance on assertions alone, however well delivered.

---

## 2. Reasoning & Interpretation

**Reviewer checks:** Is the Ask specific and matched to what the evidence supports? Is the implementation/sustainability plan realistic given the evidence and constraints already established?

| Level | Descriptor |
|---|---|
| Not Yet Evident | The Ask is vague or absent ("thank you for listening"), or the implementation plan ignores constraints and risks already identified earlier in the project. |
| Developing | An Ask exists but is generic, or the implementation plan is only loosely connected to the evidence and realities of the project. |
| Meets Standard | The Ask is specific and proportional to the evidence, and the implementation plan realistically accounts for known constraints and risks. |
| Strong Evidence | The Ask and implementation plan together make a genuinely persuasive, realistic case for a concrete next step. |

**Key test:** Reviewer should be able to complete: "Based on what we learned, they are asking for ___, which makes sense because ___."

---

## 3. Process Rigor

**Reviewer checks:** Was Mock Reviewer Q&A actually completed? Was the pitch rehearsed and timed? Does the presentation follow the required sequence (Know audience → Story → Visualize → Demonstrate → Defend → Implementation → Ask)?

| Level | Descriptor |
|---|---|
| Not Yet Evident | Mock Reviewer Q&A was not completed, or the pitch appears unrehearsed and disorganized. |
| Developing | Some preparation occurred but Q&A prep or rehearsal was minimal. |
| Meets Standard | Mock Reviewer Q&A was completed, the pitch was rehearsed and timed, and it follows a clear, logical sequence. |
| Strong Evidence | Preparation is thorough — the team anticipated tough questions accurately and the pitch sequence flows naturally from evidence to Ask. |

**Non-negotiable:** Mock Reviewer Q&A is required, not optional.

---

## 4. Integrity & Reflection

**Reviewer checks:** Does the team honestly discuss limitations and what remains unknown? Can each team member explain the project and their own specific contribution?

| Level | Descriptor |
|---|---|
| Not Yet Evident | Limitations are glossed over or hidden, or one team member appears to own the entire project while others cannot explain their role. |
| Developing | Some limitations are acknowledged, but individual accountability is uneven across the team. |
| Meets Standard | The team honestly discusses limitations and open questions, and every team member can explain both the project and their own specific contribution. |
| Strong Evidence | The team demonstrates genuine ownership — limitations are discussed candidly, and individual contributions are clear, specific, and consistent across members. |

**Non-negotiable:** Every team member must be able to explain the project and their own contribution.

---

## 5. Completeness

**Reviewer checks — the submission includes:** audience definition; evidence-based story; clear problem; research evidence; solution selection reasoning; prototype/iteration story; impact evidence; strong evidence visual; appropriate demo; claims linked to evidence; limitations; Implementation & Sustainability Model; clear Ask; reflection; future direction; Mock Reviewer Q&A completion; (HS: budget/resources, funding/support model, partnerships, risk/mitigation, executive summary; Stretch: GTM, opportunity sizing, technical architecture).

| Level | Descriptor |
|---|---|
| Not Yet Evident | Multiple required components are missing, including components necessary to make the case Finals-ready. |
| Developing | Most components are present, but one or more required pieces are incomplete or insufficient. |
| Meets Standard | All required Stage 6 submission elements are present and usable by the reviewer. |
| Strong Evidence | All elements are present and integrated into one coherent, complete Innovation Portfolio and pitch. |

---

## 6. Communication & Clarity

**Reviewer checks:** Can the reviewer follow the full six-stage story without confusion? Is the demo and visual evidence clear? Could a busy decision-maker understand the project quickly (via the executive summary, where applicable)?

| Level | Descriptor |
|---|---|
| Not Yet Evident | The reviewer cannot follow the story, evidence, or Ask without significant clarification. |
| Developing | The pitch is understandable but requires interpretation to connect evidence to conclusions. |
| Meets Standard | The story, evidence, demo, and Ask are clearly presented and easy to follow. |
| Strong Evidence | The pitch is concise, coherent, evidence-centered, and makes the full case immediately understandable to the intended audience. |

---

## Stage 6 Gate Rule

**ADVANCE / FINALS READY** — All six required dimensions must be Meets Standard or Strong Evidence, AND these non-negotiables must be present:
1. Every major claim traces to Portfolio evidence
2. Mock Reviewer Q&A was completed
3. Every team member can explain the project and their own contribution
4. The Ask is specific

Reviewer certifies: *FINALS READY — The team can make a credible, evidence-backed case for what should happen next.*

**REVISE & RESUBMIT** — If any dimension is Developing or Not Yet Evident, the student receives targeted feedback: strength → flagged dimension → specific action → resubmission expectation.

---

## Example Stage 6 Review

A team delivers a polished, well-designed pitch deck with strong visuals. During Mock Reviewer Q&A, only one team member can answer questions about the research and testing phases; the others say "she did that part." The pitch ends with "thank you for listening," with no specific Ask.

| Dimension | Rating | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Evidence Quality | Meets Standard | Claims are generally traceable to the Portfolio |
| Reasoning & Interpretation | Not Yet Evident | No Ask exists at all |
| Process Rigor | Meets Standard | Q&A prep and rehearsal occurred |
| Integrity & Reflection | Not Yet Evident | Only one member can explain most of the project |
| Completeness | Developing | Missing a stated Ask |
| Communication & Clarity | Strong Evidence | The pitch and visuals are very clear |

**Overall: REVISE & RESUBMIT.** Required action: write a specific closing Ask ("here is what we need from you"), and ensure every team member can explain the full project and their own specific contribution — not just their own section — before the next Mock Reviewer Q&A.

---

## Finals Selection Scoring (competitive, applies only after Stage 6 ADVANCE)

Teams that pass the Stage 6 gate enter Finals Selection Scoring, where the same six dimensions are used comparatively to rank teams for placement — this is the only point in the program where teams are scored against each other rather than against a fixed standard. Whether dimensions are weighted equally or unequally at Finals is decided in a separate *i3League Finals Judging Framework* document, not in this rubric.
