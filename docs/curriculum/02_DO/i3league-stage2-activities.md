# i3League — Stage 2: INVESTIGATE — Activities

## By Topic

| Topic | Activity | Worksheet | Output | Artifact Contribution |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Basic Data Literacy | Data Reality Check — review one statistic from your research and identify sample size, who was included, and whether it is representative | Evidence Tracker | 1 annotated statistic with sample-size/context notes | Research Brief |
| Bias Awareness | Bias Hunt — review your survey/interview questions and rewrite any that are leading or loaded | Bias Check | Revised unbiased questions | Research Brief / research tools |
| Data Privacy Basics | Data Minimization Review — remove any personal information you do not actually need to answer your research question | Data Privacy Check | Cleaned survey/interview instrument | Research Brief / research tools |
| Research Integrity | Contradicting Evidence Challenge — deliberately identify at least one finding that weakens, complicates, or challenges your current thinking | Evidence Tracker | 1 documented piece of challenging evidence + interpretation | Research Brief |
| Source Triangulation | Triangulate a Claim — take one major claim and support/check it using multiple evidence types | Source Triangulation Matrix | 1 key claim checked against 2–3 different evidence types | Research Brief |
| Citation vs. Plagiarism | Source Audit — identify where every non-original fact, idea, image, statistic, or quote came from and check source credibility | Source Credibility Checklist + Evidence Tracker | Completed source list + credibility review | Research Brief |
| Problem Ecosystem / System Mapping *(HS CORE)* | Map the System — identify actors, causes, constraints, feedback loops, and relationships around the problem | System Map | 1 completed problem ecosystem map | Research Brief |

## Supporting Activities (required by the Stage 2 advancement bar, not tied to a single topic heading)

| Activity | Worksheet | Output | Why it's needed |
|---|---|---|---|
| Research Question Planning | Research Question Planner | 2–4 focused research questions | Gives the investigation direction before collecting evidence |
| Evidence Collection | Survey Builder and/or Interview Question Builder | Survey/interview instrument + collected responses | Produces the actual evidence Stage 2 depends on |
| Root Cause Analysis | Five Whys / Root Cause Worksheet | Root cause chain + proposed root cause | Required by the Stage 2 advancement criteria |
| Existing Solutions & Gap Analysis | Existing Solutions & Gap Analysis | Comparison of 2–4 existing approaches + identified gap | Required before entering IMAGINE |
| Stakeholder Mapping *(all students)* | Stakeholder Map | Users, beneficiaries, decision-makers, funders/supporters, influencers | Strengthens later interviews, impact analysis, and implementation — not HS-only, unlike System Mapping |

## Stage 2 Output Rollup

Research Questions + Survey/Interview Evidence + Evidence Tracker + Source Triangulation + Root Cause Analysis + Existing Solutions & Gap Analysis + Stakeholder Map combine into the required artifact:

**Research Brief** (+ **System Map** for HS students)

**Advancement check for Stage 2** (per the frozen closing bar): student can show multiple types of evidence, honestly explain what the research found, identify a credible root cause, and explain what existing solutions currently miss.

---

**Design principle carried through this layer:** not every topic needs its own brand-new worksheet. Reusing the Evidence Tracker across data literacy, integrity, triangulation, and citation teaches students that evidence is one continuous thread, not four separate assignments.
