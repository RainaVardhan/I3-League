## Supporting Activities

| Activity | Worksheet | Output | Why it's needed |
|---|---|---|---|
| Pitch Evidence Audit | Evidence Defense Sheet + Innovation Portfolio Checklist | Every major pitch claim traced to portfolio evidence | Prevents unsupported storytelling |
| Pitch Rehearsal & Timing | Demo Planner + Story Arc Builder | Full timed rehearsal | Ensures students can deliver within competition limits |
| Mock Reviewer Q&A *(required)* | Tough Questions / Q&A Prep | Reviewer feedback + revised answers | Tests whether students can truly defend the work |
| Final Ask Test | Ask Builder + Audience Map | Audience-appropriate final Ask | Ensures the pitch ends in a real decision |

**Mock Reviewer Q&A is required, not optional.** A polished pitch deck should not qualify a team for Finals if they cannot answer: How do you know? What didn't work? What are your limitations? Why did you choose this solution? What would happen next?

## Stage 6 Output Rollup

Audience Map + Story Arc + Evidence Visuals + Demo + Evidence Defense + Q&A Preparation + Implementation & Sustainability Model + Clear Ask + Reflection combine into the required artifact:

**Final Pitch + Innovation Portfolio**

For HS students, additionally: Budget/Resources + Funding/Support + Partnerships + Risk/Mitigation + Executive Summary

Stretch work can add: Adoption/GTM + Opportunity Sizing + Technical Architecture

## Advancement / Finals Readiness Check

A student or team should be able to show:
- a clear problem-to-impact story
- evidence supporting every major claim
- a working demonstration or appropriate demonstration of the solution
- clear visuals communicating important evidence
- honest discussion of limitations and what remains unknown
- ability to answer critical questions without bluffing
- realistic implementation and sustainability thinking
- a complete Innovation Portfolio
- a specific next-step Ask
- clear ownership of what was learned and what should happen next

**And:** every team member must be able to explain the project and their contribution — protecting against one student doing everything while the rest simply appear at Finals.

---

## Required Sequence

**Know the Audience → Build the Evidence Story → Visualize → Demonstrate → Defend → Plan Implementation → Define the Ask → Rehearse → Face Questions → Revise → Present → Decide What Comes Next**

**Final i3League principle:** The goal is not to sound impressive. The goal is to make a credible case for what should happen next.
