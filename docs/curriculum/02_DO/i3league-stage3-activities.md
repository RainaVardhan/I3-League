# i3League — Stage 3: IMAGINE — Activities

## By Topic

| Topic | Activity | Worksheet | Output | Artifact Contribution |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Value Proposition | Value in One Sentence — complete "Someone would use this because…" and refine until it names a real user benefit | Value Proposition Builder | 1 clear value proposition | Concept Portfolio |
| User Needs vs. Features | Feature Trace — connect every proposed feature to a real need from Stages 1–2 and remove weak ones | Needs vs. Features Map | Need-to-feature map | Concept Portfolio |
| Divergent Thinking / Solution Exploration | Three Different Ways — generate at least 3 genuinely different solution approaches, not variations of one idea | Idea Generation Sheet | 3+ distinct concepts | Concept Portfolio |
| Constraints Thinking | Reality Check — identify time, money, policy, skills, materials, technology, or other limits that affect each concept | Constraints Checklist | Constraints list by concept | Concept Portfolio |
| Risk Identification | Pre-Mortem — imagine the idea failed and identify the most likely reasons why | Risk Register | Top risks + early warning signs | Concept Portfolio |
| Accessibility & Inclusion | Who Might Be Left Out? — identify users or groups the solution could exclude and revise accordingly | Accessibility & Inclusion Check | 1+ accessibility considerations | Concept Portfolio |
| Responsible / Ethical Design | Could This Cause Harm? — identify misuse, unfairness, privacy, safety, or unintended-effect risks | Ethical Design Check | Ethical concerns + response | Concept Portfolio |
| AI Fit & Responsible Use | Does This Need AI? — answer what AI adds, what could go wrong, what data it needs, and whether a simpler solution could work | AI Fit Check | AI fit decision + reasoning | Concept Portfolio |
| Assumption Prioritization | Assumption Hunt — list what must be true for the selected concept to work | Assumption Map | Assumption list | Concept Portfolio |
| Build vs. Buy vs. Partner *(HS CORE)* | Make or Leverage? — identify what should be built, adapted, purchased, or supported through partnership | Build vs. Buy vs. Partner | Build/buy/partner decision | Concept Portfolio |
| Originality, Attribution & IP | What Exists Already? — document inspirations, existing solutions, adapted elements, original contributions, and attribution needs | Originality, Attribution & IP Record | Originality / attribution record | Concept Portfolio |
| Decision Matrix / Concept Selection | Compare Before You Commit — score the concepts against shared criteria such as impact, feasibility, cost, responsibility, and fit | Decision Matrix | Completed concept comparison + selected concept | Concept Portfolio |

## Supporting Activity

| Activity | Worksheet | Output | Why it's needed |
|---|---|---|---|
| Critical Assumption Selection | Critical Assumption Prioritizer | 1 named critical assumption + explanation of why it matters most | Creates the direct handoff into ITERATE |

## Stage 3 Output Rollup

Value Proposition + Need/Feature Map + 3+ Concepts + Constraints + Risks + Accessibility/Ethics + AI Fit + Assumptions + Originality/IP record + Decision Matrix combine into the required artifact:

**Concept Portfolio**, with the final selected concept and its **Critical Assumption** — the bridge to Stage 4.

**Advancement check for Stage 3** (per the frozen closing bar): student can show multiple genuinely different concepts, a reasoned concept selection, a completed decision matrix, a named critical assumption, an honest account of what already exists, what was adapted or credited, and what is distinct about the selected concept.

---

## Required Sequence

Students must not select their concept before completing the decision matrix — otherwise the matrix becomes paperwork used to justify a decision already made. The required order is:

**Explore → Compare → Check responsibility/originality → Decide → Identify critical assumption → Advance**
