# i3League — Stage 1: INSIGHT — Activities

| Topic | Activity | Worksheet | Output | Artifact Contribution |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Observation Skills | 3-Day Friction Hunt — over three days, notice and record 5–15 moments of friction, confusion, delay, or workaround | Friction Log | 5–15 logged observations | Stage 1 Evidence Notes |
| Empathy Without Assumption | Empathy Interview — talk to one person affected by the problem and record their experience in their own words, without inserting your own interpretation | Empathy Interview Guide | 1 completed interview record | Stage 1 Evidence Notes |
| Problem Scope | Scope Narrowing Drill — take a broad problem and narrow it by naming who, where, and when, until it's specific enough to act on | Problem Scope Worksheet | 1 scoped problem statement | Validated Problem Statement |
| Ethical Interviewing & Consent | Consent Script Practice — write and rehearse a one-sentence intro explaining the interview's purpose and the person's right to skip or stop | Interview Consent Script | 1 written consent intro | Used before every Stage 1–2 interview |
| Team Charter | Team Agreement Session — as a team, agree on roles, check-in frequency, and how disagreements will be resolved | Team Charter | 1 signed team agreement | Stage 1 Evidence Notes |

**Stage 1 output rollup:** Friction Log + Empathy Interview Guide + Problem Scope Worksheet combine into the **Problem Statement Builder**, which produces the stage's required artifact: **Validated Problem Statement + Evidence Notes.**

**Advancement check for Stage 1** (per the frozen closing bar): a specific affected group is identified, the problem is not a disguised solution, at least one firsthand source exists, evidence supports the problem, and the student can explain why it matters.
